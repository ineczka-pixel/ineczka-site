Handmade

Paintings from CD-Disks

A disk is frozen light. I transform its reflections into what glows in reality: feathers, scales, water, metal — and open doors to worlds that don't exist.

Each work is an appliqué made from cut fragments of CD-disks, where holographic flares convey what paint cannot: the shine of metal, the iridescence of wings, the reflection of water in the sun. Precise graphics and fragile, "impossible" materials together create the feeling of looking into another, fantastic world — a ready-made art object for the home or a gift that can't be confused with anything else.

Some works from the author's personal archive — for current gallery and prices, inquire directly.
